Families

What is family?

  • Family is the most universal social organization.
  • Family as Ancient Social Unit.
  • Historically, in hunter-gather era, all events (give birth, food produce, trade items and power assignment) happends in unit of family.

家庭是一个最普遍、最古老的社会组织形式（social organization）

自狩猎时代起，所有的生活事务——育儿、食物生产、贸易、权力分配——都是在家庭单位内进行

Inequality @ Domestic Labor

Nowadays, female are still responsible for primary daily grind.

Reproductive Labor

  • Reproductive Labor - (Feminist term)
    • include not only give birth to children, but also all work needs to be done to maintain and ‘reproduce’ manpower (human) like Cook, Clean, Take care of children and elderly.
  • Labor of Love
    • society often embellish these work as ‘The Labor of Love’, which indicates that females are voluntary for doing these, not a valued ‘work’.
  • Invisible work
    • These works are often INVISIBLE - they are not officially considered economic devotion, not getting paid, and always be negelicted until nobody responsible for them (for example, no one at home shopping or clean)

观点 ： 女性仍然承担着最主要的日常繁重家务，这是不平等的体现。

对此， 女权主义提出了一个叫做【再生产劳动】（Reproductive Labor）的概念，他指的不仅仅是生孩子，而是所有为了维持和“再生产”劳动力（人）所必需的工作，例如洗衣，做饭，打扫卫生，照顾孩子和老人。

而社会常常把这些女性的Reproductive Labor美化为“爱の劳动”，暗示这些工作都是女性出于自愿而做的，不是一种“有价值”的工作。（虽然这个自愿本人可能都不知道）

此外，这些Reproductive Labor通常都是无形的（Invisible），这些工作不被视为真正的经济贡献，而且也没有报酬，常常被人忽视。直到没有人做的时候才会发现。（例如，妈妈出差的时候，家里没人买菜 没人打扫卫生，直到这时才会发现妈妈做家务劳动时的付出。）

Females are facing Double Shift

  • Double shift
    • Women have burden not only at work outside (do job to get paid), but also at home (the majority of housework and raise the children - without getting paid, which is the second burden)
    • Double shift points out that females are facing dilemma like this.
    • FLAWS - this double shift limits women’s options of their lives, for example, they may not able to have occupation promotion, no time to improve or entertaining themselfves - which might cause overload physcially or mentally.

观点 ： 女性面临着双重负担

它指的是，当女性既要在外工作（从事有偿劳动，这是第一重负担），回家后又要承担绝大多数的家务和育儿责任（从事无偿的再生产劳动，这是第二重负担）时，她们所面临的困境。

这种双重负担限制了女性的选择 (limits women’s options)，比如她们可能无法寻求职业晋升、没有时间进行自我提升或休闲，导致身心俱疲。

Marriage, Economics, Control, Power

Marriage isn’t only about love, but a complex combination of economics and social rules. It also related to economics, control and assignment of power.

Marriage has changed a lot with the development of society. Take Canada as an example in historical evolution :

  • 1950s - Companionate marriage
    • The companionate marriage mainly focus on strict gendered roles - Husbands must be breadwinners while Wifes must be homemakers.
  • Modern - Individualized marriage
    • Modern marriage more focus on self-development and personal happiness.
    • Problem Exists - However, although the view of marriage have changed from companionate to individualized, the DOUBLE SHIFT of female still persistes.

Data Analysis

Women = +1 month housework/year

If we quantify the unpaid work (house work, etc.) done by women, it equals for women to work one month more than men annually.

观点 ： 除了爱情之外，婚姻是一种经济和社会制度，涉及到经济、控制和权力的分配

在加拿大，从1950年至今，婚姻形式从陪伴型婚姻（男耕女织）到如今的个体化婚姻，体现了时代的进步。但是双重负担依然存在

Women += 1 month housework /year 这一条数据是为了量化“双重负担”。它意味着，如果把女性每年比男性多做的所有无偿家务劳动时间加起来，相当于她们每年要比男性多工作整整一个月。

Overall..

​ Although the form of marriage have become more modern, the inequality still persists in housework and other areas, which contributes to the women’s Double Shift. And the essence of that is the Economics and Power Inequality.

总之 尽管社会和婚姻形式已经现代化，但家务劳动的性别不平等依然顽固存在，这构成了对女性的“双重负担”，其本质是一种经济和权力的不平等

Nuclear Family

Unclear family

  • Unclear Family - A family consisting of parents and children
  • The term of Unclear Family was invented at 1950s, at that time, it was promoted as TIMELESS ideal model.
  • The term of Unclear Family overlooked many othe society forms in which EXTENDED Family (multi-gen under one roof ) is considered normal.
  • Famous Feminist Bell Hooks (1981) : European model doesn’t fit all. Which highlights the limitation of cultural standars.

1950年代，核心家庭 （ Nuclear Family - 只有父母和孩子组成的家庭 ）的概念出现，在当时被认为是【永恒的，自古以来便正确，并且一直沿用至今】的理想模型。

但是 这种说法忽略了其他的许多社会形态，在那些被忽略的社会形态中【大家庭（Extended Family - 即几代人同住一个屋檐下的家庭）】才是常态。

注明女权主义者 Bell hooks强调 “欧洲中心主义的家庭模式并不适用于所有人” (European model doesn’t fit all)。这强调了“核心家庭”理想是一种具有文化局限性的标准。

State-Engineered Nuclear Ideal

Canada enforce this Nuclear Ideal by legislation and setting immigration policy.

REAL LIFE CASE : Live-in Caregiver Program (1992-2024)

  • Aim
  • Function
  • Effect
  • Change

The Global Care Chain refers to the cross-border transfer and connection of caregiving labor, where female immigrants take on caregiving work such as childcare, elderly care, or housework, transferring caregiving responsibilities that were originally within the family to others, thereby forming a global caregiving relationship chain.

加拿大 通过立法 (legislation) 和移民政策 (immigration) 来强行推行这种“核心家庭”模式

同时 【住家保姆计划 （Live-in Caregiver Program）1992-2014 】作为典型案例。该计划超过90%的参与者都是来自菲律宾和加勒比地区的女性，他们被要求将自己的孩子留在她们的母国，而来加拿大照顾百人家庭的孩子。

该政策一方面允许加拿大的核心家庭（通常是白人）得以维持（因为有人帮他们承担了【再生产劳动】的任务），另一方面 强行拆散了这些移民妇女自己的家庭

直到2014年，这项不人道的live-in住家规则才被废除

Colonial Devastation of Indigenous Families

The Canadian government’s colonial policies systematically destroyed Indigenous family structures.

  • Indian Act 1876
    • Indian acts rules that - If Indigenous female married with a non-Native male, she will lost her Indigenous status.
    • Indian Act is a form of legislative intervention based on gender and race. It defineds who belongs to Indigenous by control the marriage, and deprives women of their identity and family belonging.
  • Residential Schools
    • The child of Indigenous are forced to taken away from home (Kids stolen), which caused serious Intergenerational Trauma.
    • The reason why it called Intergenerational Trauma is because the traumas (abuse, loss of culture and language, seperate with family) will effect current generation to pass trauma to their next generation. This have negative effect on their next gen even they dind’t go through these events on their own.
  • 1985 Amendments
    • After the Sandra Lovelace UN case, this was bill finally amended.
    • tip : Sandra Lovelace, an Indigenous woman who lost her identity after marrying a non-Indigenous man, took her case to the UN Human Rights Committee and ultimately won, forcing Canada to amend discriminatory clauses in the Indian Act. This once again demonstrates that family identity is a focal point of political struggle.

加拿大政府的殖民政策系统性地破坏了原住民家庭结构。

其中，最重要的就是1876印第安法案、寄宿学校和1985修正案。

  • ① 1876印第安法案
    • 法案规定，如果原住民妇女与非原住民 (non-Native) 男性结婚，她将失去其原住民身份。
    • 这是一种基于性别和种族的立法干预，它通过控制婚姻来强行定义谁“属于”原住民社群，并剥夺了妇女的身份认同和家庭归属权。
  • ② 寄宿学校
    • 原住民儿童被强行从家中带走 (Kids stolen)。这造成了严重的跨代创伤 (intergenerational trauma)。
      • “跨代创伤”是指创伤（如虐待、语言和文化的丧失、与家人的分离）的影响会从经历创伤的一代人传递给她们的后代，即使后代没有亲身经历过这些事件。
  • ③ 1985修正案
    • 在 Sandra Lovelace 联合国案 (Sandra Lovelace UN case) 之后，该法案才被修正。
      • Sandra Lovelace 是一名原住民妇女，她因嫁给非原住民而失去了身份。她将此案告到了联合国人权委员会，并最终胜诉，迫使加拿大修改了《印第安法案》中的歧视性条款。这再次证明了家庭身份是政治斗争的焦点。

Nuclear Family in Decline

According to the data from StatsCan, the form of Nuclear Family have greatly declined in these years.

  • 2001 → 2011

    • The percentage of Traditional Unclear Family have declined from 37.4% to 31.9%

    • The percentage of Common-law increased by 13.9%

      • Tip: “Common-law” refers to a relationship where two people live together and have a marriage-like relationship, but are not legally married. In Canada, after living together for a certain period (usually a year), both parties legally enjoy the same rights and obligations as a married couple.

    • The number of Same-sex Marriage have tripled (x3)

    • The percentage of Single-parent increased by 8%

      • Tip: 80% of the single parents are mothers.

These data clearly demonstrate that family patterns in Canada are becoming more diverse, while “traditional” patterns are declining.

加拿大统计局的数据证明了，传统核心家庭在社会中的占比正在下降。

从2001到2011年，传统核心家庭的占比从 37.4% 降到了 31.9% ，同时，事实婚姻（同居伴侣）的比例增长了 13.9% ，同性婚姻增长了三倍，而单亲家庭的比例也增长了8%（其中80%的单亲家长是母亲）

这些数据清晰地表明，加拿大的家庭模式正在变得更加多样化，而“传统”模式正在减少。

2021 Census

Comparing the data between 1981 and 2021:

  • The percentage of couple with child have droped from 64% (1981) to 50%
  • The number of Trans/non-bionary couple have first reached more than 32000.
  • Now, In single parents family, the percentage of child living with father are now 21% which is higher than before 14% (this also updates the stereotype of 80% of single parent is mother, which shows that gender dynamics within single-parent families are also changing. )
  • 35% Young adult (20~34 years old) choose to live with their parents.

截止2021年 有孩子的伴侣从1981年的 64% 大幅下降到 50%；加拿大统计局首次统计到超过 32000 对 跨性别/非二元性别伴侣；单亲父亲的比例提升，打破了单亲母亲的认知；35%的年轻成年人选择与父母同住

​ Family patterns (such as those with children, without children, and transgender couples) are becoming increasingly diverse. At the same time, economic factors (such as high housing prices and unstable employment) may lead more young people to be unable to live independently and choose to live with their parents (which is closer to the “extended family” model than the “nuclear family” model).

家庭模式（如有孩、无孩、跨性别伴侣）日益多样化。同时，经济因素（如高房价和不稳定的就业）可能导致了更多年轻人无法独立，选择与父母同住（这更接近“大家庭”模式，而非“核心家庭”模式）。

Alternatives

The nuclear family has never been the only form; other forms of life have always existed throughout history. There are many Alternatives:

  • Historical Alternatives:

    • Beguines, 12 century
      • This was a medieval women’s labor collective. The women did not marry, lived and worked together, and were economically and spiritually self-sufficient.
    • Lowell Mills, 1820s
      • During the Industrial Revolution, factory girls left their families and lived in boarding houses.
    • Utopias
      • Drawing on the concept of the “Republic” by the ancient Greek philosopher Plato, he proposed the idea of communal child-rearing, in which children are raised by the whole society, rather than by a single family.

  • Modern Alternatives:

    • Child-free by Choice
      • This refers to a family model where members voluntarily choose not to have children (referring to the viewpoint of scholar Christine Overall).
    • Women living alone
      • Data shows that 84% of women living alone help their parents, compared to only 68% of married women. This challenges the stereotype that “married women are more family-oriented.”

  • Core Pre-Conditions

    • Freedom requires Income
    • No job, no choice

以上内容反驳了“核心家庭是唯一模式”的观点，指出历史上一直存在其他生活形式

  • 贝居安修会 (Beguines, 12世纪): 这是一个中世纪的妇女劳动集体 (Women’s labor collectives)。女性们不结婚，共同生活和工作，在经济和精神上自给自足。

  • 洛厄尔纺织厂 (Lowell Mills, 1820s): 在工业革命期间，工厂女孩 (Factory girls) 离开家庭，住在集体宿舍 (boarding houses) 里。

  • 乌托邦 (Utopias): 引用了古希腊哲学家柏拉图 (Plato) 的“理想国”概念，他提出了社区共同抚养 (communal child-rearing) 的想法，即孩子由整个社会共同抚养，而不是由单一家庭。

  • 丁克 (Child-free by choice): 指自愿选择不生孩子的家庭模式（引用了学者 Christine Overall 的观点）。

  • 独居女性 (Women living alone): 数据显示，独居女性中有 84% 的人会帮助父母，而相比之下，已婚女性只有 68%。这挑战了“已婚女性更顾家”的刻板印象。

New Family Forms

Nowadays, there are many different types of family forms:

  • Blended : family have step-parents
  • Single-parents :
  • Transnational : Fillpina Moms
  • Chosen Families : Family formed in LGBTQ+ groups (especially in AIDS era - when they are excluded by biology family, they form family with friends or other who have no blood connection but provide care and help.)

列举了一些当今社会中正在增长的、多样的家庭结构，以证明“核心家庭”不再是主流

  • 重组家庭 (Blended): 即有继父或继母 (step-parents) 的家庭。
  • 单亲家庭 (Single-parent): 指出 80% 的单亲家长是母亲，且她们的经济状况通常更差 (poorer)。
  • 跨国家庭 (Transnational): 再次提到那个例子，比如菲律宾妈妈 (Filipina moms) 在加拿大工作，而她们的孩子在别处（如菲律宾）生活。
  • 选择的家庭 (Chosen families): 这是一个重要概念，特指在 LGBTQ+ 群体中（尤其是在艾滋病 (AIDS) 流行时代）形成的家庭。当他们被原生家庭 (biology family) 排斥时，他们会与朋友、伴侣等组成没有血缘关系但提供关怀和支持的家庭网络。
  • 同性家庭 (Same-sex): 引用了2011年的数据（64,575对），并指出其增长迅速（增长了 42%）。

Conclusion

  • Ineuqality still exists:

    • Single moms annual income $42k is significantly lower than couple family $100K

    • Caregivers : they send 70% of their income back to their home

  • Example:

    • Q : Why do 80% of fathers in Quebec take paternity leave, compared to less than 30% in the rest of Canada?
    • A : Because Quebec have QPIP (Quebec Parental Insurance Plan)
    • This plan provided fathers with exclusive, non-transferable leave, which was “use it or lose it.” This meant that if the father didn’t take the leave, it was wasted, and the mother couldn’t take it for him.
    • Result: This policy greatly promoted paternal involvement in childcare. This perfectly demonstrates how “state policy” can “construct” family behavior.

  • Family = Verb, Not Noun: This is the most important conclusion. The family is not a static “thing” (noun), but a dynamic “process” (verb) that needs to be maintained, negotiated, and practiced.

  • The family is socially constructed: It is shaped by three factors:

    • State policy (e.g., QPIP, Indian Act)

    • Economics (e.g., income inequality, caregivers)

    • Culture (e.g., the “ideal family” propaganda of the 1950s)

不平等依旧存在，因此，我们需要努力减少不平等

家庭 = 动词，不是名词 (Family = Verb, Not Noun): 这是最重要的结论。家庭不是一个静止的“东西”（名词），而是一个需要被维持、协商和实践的动态“过程”（动词）。

  • 家庭是被社会建构的 (Family is socially constructed): 它被以下三个因素共同塑造：
    1. 国家政策 (State policy) (如 QPIP, 《印第安法案》)
    2. 经济 (Economics) (如收入差距, 护工)
    3. 文化 (Culture) (如 1950年代的“理想家庭”宣传)
