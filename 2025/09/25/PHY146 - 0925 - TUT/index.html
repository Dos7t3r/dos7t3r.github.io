1. Uncertainty & Error Propagation

3. Lab Reports

this parts contains something needs to pay attention to about the Physics Lab Report

Significant Figures (SigFigs)

  • Will take off 0.5 for incorrect SIGH FIGs
    • 9.81234 with error of 0.02568 → 9.81±0.03

Length

5 pages (exclude first page and appendix)

  • Over will take off 0.5 points (over 10 will cause off of 1.0 point)

Appendix

  • Inclued figures, tables, raw data, sample calculations that improve the understanding of your lab
  • Clear and organized.

Rubric

Quercus

  • Raw data presented in an appropriate format (usually a concise table with headings, units, a number, and caption) including errors of the raw measurements.

  • Graphs and figures to summarize the data, with appropriate annotations (like a line of best fit), error bars, and labels and titles. Graphs made in Python. There is a significant amount of coherent narrative present, not just graphs.

  • Error analysis for derived quantities, like a calculated slope of a line or anything else calculated but not directly measured. Should include a specific ‘derivation’ for your error, and example error calculations clearly reasoned. Based on this error analysis you should report your final answer appropriately rounded. Sample calculations are present.

What’s more..

  • TA will grading off the lab guidelines from the quercus.

  • TA looking for absolute statements about results. i.e. whether you have verified or not verified the known value within uncertainty

  • All results should have a + - uncertainty, but no need to calculate % error.

  • Refer to plots and equations in discussion/conclusion, if you don’t, then they probably belong in your appendix.

  • DON’T STATE HUMAN ERROR as your source of error for the lab. You want to demonstrate that you did the lab carefully and properly.
