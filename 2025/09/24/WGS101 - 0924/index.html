Intersectionality

Intersectionality，交叉法，交错性。

What is intersectionality?

  • Identity aspects overlap, shaping unique experiences
  • Multiple oppressions intersect, creating distinct realities
  • Analyzing identity factors beyond sex and gender
  • Understanding power dynamics and diverse impacts.

[!NOTE]

Intersectionality - 交错性 意味着

  • 身份方面重叠，不同身份因素（性别、种族、阶级、性取向等）相互重叠，塑造了独特的个人经历。

  • 多重压迫（如性别歧视、种族歧视）交织在一起，创造出复杂的社会现实

  • 分析需要超越性别和性别的身份因素，不局限于单一维度（例如只看性别）

  • 了解权力动态和各种影响，综合多种身份与权力关系分析。

Historical Context

1. Sojourner Truth

Sojourner Truth（苏珍娜·特鲁斯，1797-1883）

  • Sojourner Truth’s critique: Race overlooked in first-wave feminism
  • Emergence of contemporary intersectionality in the 1990s.
  • Women of color excluded from mainstream feminism.

[!NOTE]

Sojourner Truth 人物生平

  • 1851 年在俄亥俄州发表著名演讲 “Ain’t I A Woman?”。

  • 指出黑人女性既遭受性别歧视也遭受种族压迫，却常被排除在白人女性主义话语之外。

  • 她用亲身经历质疑“女性需要特别照顾”的说法，强调黑人女性同样有力量和劳动能力。

20 世纪 90 年代：

  • 有色人种女性批判主流女性主义只代表白人中产阶级女性，推动了“交叉性”概念的发展。

2. Kimberlé Crenshaw

  • Lawsuit against General Motors
  • Forced choice: racism or sexism?
  • Court’s denial of grounds for discrimination claim.
  • Overlooked uniqueness of black women’s experiences.

[!NOTE]

在研究美国通用汽车（General Motors）的诉讼案例时提出交叉性。

法院要求原告选择是“种族歧视”还是“性别歧视”，却忽视了黑人女性的独特处境。

说明传统法律和社会分析无法处理多重身份压迫的交织性。

Beyond Single-Axis Analysis

Shifts away from one-dimensional analysis.

Emphasis on transcending oppositional views of inequality.

Complex Power Dynamics and Oppression

  • Simultaneous conditions of privilege and oppression.

  • “Race, class, sexuality, and so on each intersect with each other and create simultaneous conditions of privilege and oppression” (p.59-60).

  • “Intersectionality demonstrates how, under certain conditions, oppression and privilege can be simultaneously experienced” (p. 62).

[!NOTE]

超越单一轴线分析：不再是“要么是性别，要么是种族”，而是同时考虑。

复杂的权力动态：

  • 一个人可能同时在某些方面享有特权，同时在另一些方面受到压迫。
  • 引文强调了种族、阶级、性取向等身份之间相互作用。

Audre Lorde（奥德丽·洛德）：

  • 提出 “没有压迫的等级制” (No Hierarchy of Oppressions)，即各种压迫不可相互比较和排序。
  • 相关视频：https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-CPAlSVaiL8&ab_channel=AfroMarxist

Assignment

Annotated Bibliographies and research proposals

对于WGS第一个作业——这里有一些问答

请勿直接引用（direct quote）！！！不允许直接把别人的话弄过来,只可以reference！

What do you have to do?

  1. Select a theme relating to women and gender studies that you wish to research and critically examine.
    2.Write an annotated bibliography
  • 1 page, 2-3 paragraphs, consistent citation style, etc.
  1. mnpeer-reviewed sources that are important to the understanding of the topic
  • Brief and accurate summaries of the main arguments of each source
  1. Write a research proposal
  • 2-3 pages (excluding bib)
  • 1 page introduction-context and theory, thesis, research questions, roadmap
  • 1 page expansion on theories
  • Discussion of the conceptual considerations

summarize - the contributions and why they matter

  • You summarize the contributions and why they matter

    Key points you might summarize:

    • ﻿﻿The authors research question; the thing they were trying to find out by conducting their research (introductory sections)
    • ﻿﻿The authors’ method; what they did to find that thing out / answer their research questions (methods sections, though often can be found in the Intro). Be succinct in this section!

    The author’s results; what they found after applying their method (result section, though you can find the most important findings in the Discussion section) The significance of the results; how the results bring something new to the scholarly conversation (Discussion section)
