Audre Lorde’s speech

Audre Lorde - There Is No Hierarchy Of Oppressions

Take notes while watching and pay attention to following:

  • What aspects of identity does she highlight (race, gender, class, religion)?
  • Who is she addressing?
  • What systems of power is she critiquing?

[!NOTE]

她强调的身份面向

  • 她突出了种族（race）、性别（gender）、阶级（class）、**宗教或性取向（religion/sexual orientation）**等多重身份。
  • 她以黑人（Black）、女性（woman）、女同性恋（lesbian）、女权主义者（feminist）、社会主义者（socialist）、诗人（poet）、**母亲（mother）以及跨种族伴侣成员（member of an interracial couple）**的身份发声。

She highlights multiple aspects of identity: race, gender, class, and religion/sexual orientation. She speaks as a Black woman, lesbian, feminist, socialist, poet, mother, and member of an interracial couple.

2️⃣ 她所面对的受众

  • 她主要面向其他被压迫群体（如黑人、女性、同性恋者、跨性别群体等），以及致力于社会正义与平等的人。
  • 她呼吁大家不要因不同身份而分裂，要团结起来对抗压迫。

She addresses other oppressed groups (such as Black people, women, lesbians, gay men, etc.) and all who work for social justice and equality, urging them to unite rather than divide.

3️⃣ 她批判的权力体系

  • 她批判了种族主义（racism）、性别歧视（sexism）、异性恋中心主义（heterosexism），并指出它们都源于同样的权力逻辑：
    “一种自认为优越的群体认为自己有统治其他人的权利”。
  • 她同时批评了**右翼保守势力（right-wing conservatism）**及相关法律（如 Family Protection Act），认为它们反黑人、反女性、反同性恋。

She critiques racism, sexism, and heterosexism, showing how they all come from the same belief in one group’s right to dominate others. She also criticizes right-wing conservative systems and laws like the Family Protection Act for being anti-Black, anti-woman, and anti-gay.

WRITING EXERCISE

  • Write a short paragraph (5-6 sentences) using Sojourner Truth’s speech to practice intersectional analysis. Explain how she shows that race and gender cannot be separated when analyzing women’s oppression.

  • What does her example reveal about the limits of focusing only on “women” as a single category?

AI Response EXAMPLE

[!NOTE]

Sojourner Truth’s speech “Ain’t I a Woman?” shows that race and gender cannot be separated when analyzing women’s oppression. She points out that while white women are treated with delicacy—helped into carriages and protected—Black women like herself receive no such respect and must labor like men. By describing her strength in ploughing, planting, and enduring physical punishment, she proves that the experience of a Black woman is different from that of a white woman. Truth’s repeated question, “Ain’t I a woman?” challenges the idea that womanhood is defined only by white, middle-class ideals. Her example reveals that focusing on “women” as a single category hides how racism and sexism work together to shape distinct realities for women of color. Intersectionality thus helps us understand that gender oppression is never one-dimensional.

解释要点

  • 种族与性别不可分割：她的黑人女性身份让她既面对种族主义又面对性别歧视。
  • 反驳“单一女性经验”：白人女性被保护的“脆弱”形象不能代表所有女性。
  • 交叉性核心：只有将种族与性别等多重身份一起分析，才能看见黑人女性的真实处境。

Self-Check

Underline the thesis statement of your paragraph.

Look at the sentence you underlined in your paragraph. Ask yourself:

  • Does this sentence take a clear position?
  • Is it specific (not just “Truth talks about women” but how she talks about race and gender)?
  • Does it show why it matters for feminist analysis?
  • Revise your sentence so it works as a thesis statement. In other words, it becomes a sentence that you could build a whole paper around.

Thesis Statement

STRONG VS WEAK THESIS STATEMENT

A good thesis statement raises how or why questions. In other words, it is debatable. If your thesis statement answers a what question you need to reformulate it. Remember your thesis statement will guide the entire paper.

A THESIS STATEMENT IS LIKE A PROMISE TO THE READER AND PROVE IN THE REST OF PAPER. ALAYWAS TRY TO CLEARIFY YOUR IDEA TO READERS.

Example:

  • ❌ Technology is useful in many ways and has changed our lives a lot.

[!WARNING]

Not clear in HOW and WHY

VS

  • ✅ Advancements in technology have significantly improved communication, healthcare, and education, making daily life more efficient; however, over-reliance on digital tools can also reduce critical thinking and in-person social interactions, requiring a balanced approach to its use

[!TIP]

Declare “Many Ways” and “Changed”, like how do they changed, in what part in our life,

In this example, the description of USEFUL IN MANY WAYS is much more clear because it includes considerate and diverse field in life (communication, healthcare, and education) ,and in CHANGED OUR LIVES A LOT part, it said “MAKEING DAILY LIFE MORE EFFICIENT”, which is a detail of WHAT PART.
