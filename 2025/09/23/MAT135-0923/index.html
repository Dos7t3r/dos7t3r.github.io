介值定理

介值定理，Intermediate Value Theorem.

If f(x) is continuous on [a,b], and z is between f(a) and f(b), then f(x) = z has (at least) a solution(root) c exists in [a,b].

如果f(x)在定义域[a,b]连续，并且z在f(a)和f(b)之间，那么f(c)=z的c在[a,b]内至少有一个根。
